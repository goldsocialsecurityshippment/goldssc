Gold Social Security Shipment Company is a trusted name in international logistics, delivering secure, reliable shipments across more than 190 countries since 2003.

Our Story

Founded in 2003, Gold Social Security Shipment Company began as a regional courier service focused on delivering documents and small parcels within Australia. Over two decades, we have grown into a global logistics provider operating across six continents.

Our growth has been driven by a simple principle: treat every shipment as if it were our own. This philosophy has earned the trust of millions of customers — from individuals sending gifts to family abroad, to multinational corporations managing complex supply chains.

Today, our network includes air, sea, and ground freight capabilities, with dedicated facilities in key trade hubs including Sydney, Hong Kong, Dubai, London, and New York.

Global Reach

We operate in 190+ countries through a combination of wholly-owned offices and carefully vetted local partners. This hybrid model allows us to offer the reliability of a global carrier with the expertise of local knowledge.

Our customs brokerage team has expertise in import/export regulations across all major jurisdictions, ensuring your shipments clear customs efficiently and compliantly.
